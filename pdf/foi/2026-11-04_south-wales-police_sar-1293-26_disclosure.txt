Data Protection Act, 2018 – Subject Access Application

Thank you for your request made under Article 15 of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

You have requested:
• Records created in handling FOI 719/26 and Internal Review 1188/26;
• Records created in handling FOI 1040/26;
• Records created in handling FOI 1189/26;
• Records created in handling FOI 1264/26;
• Records created in handling your email dated 28 September 2026; and
• Any internal emails, notes, discussions, comments, assessments or correspondence referring to you between 1 June 2026 and the date of our searches.

We have carefully considered this aspect of your request under Article 15 UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

The right of access entitles an individual to obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning them is being processed and, where appropriate, access to that personal data. It is not a general right of access to documents, files, correspondence, internal communications, administrative records or information simply because an individual is mentioned within them.

FOI 719/26 and Internal Review 1188/26
We have identified records generated in the handling of FOI 719/26 and Internal Review 1188/26. These records consist principally of internal communications, administrative actions, case management activity, staff discussions and consideration of the Force's statutory obligations in responding to those requests.
While some of these records contain references to you, the records themselves are primarily concerned with the administration and handling of the requests rather than the recording, evaluation or use of information about you as an individual. Any personal data relating to you is incidental to the primary purpose of the records.
To the extent that personal data relating to you is contained within these records, we have considered whether it can reasonably be extracted and disclosed. We are satisfied that any such data is inextricably linked with personal data relating to members of staff and other individuals, together with internal discussions, advice, opinions and deliberations. Disclosure would adversely affect the rights and freedoms of those individuals. Accordingly, Article 15(4) UK GDPR applies.

FOI 1040/26
We have identified records generated in the handling of FOI 1040/26. Those records are administrative and operational in nature and primarily concern how the request was considered, managed and responded to by the Force. Although the records may contain references to you, they are not principally records about you. Rather, they are records documenting the Force's consideration and handling of the request.
We have considered whether any personal data relating to you can be separated and disclosed. We are satisfied that meaningful disclosure is not possible without revealing personal data relating to other individuals and internal deliberative material. Disclosure would therefore adversely affect the rights and freedoms of others and is restricted by Article 15(4) UK GDPR.

FOI 1189/26
We have identified records generated in the handling of FOI 1189/26. The records comprise internal correspondence, administrative activity and discussions concerning the management and response to the request. Any personal data relating to you within these records forms only a limited part of wider information concerning the handling of the request. The records are not, in substance, records about you but records concerning the Force's internal processes.
We have considered whether any personal data can reasonably be extracted and disclosed. We are satisfied that such information cannot be separated from staff personal data and internal discussions without adversely affecting the rights and freedoms of others. Article 15(4) UK GDPR therefore applies.

FOI 1264/26
We have identified records generated in the handling of FOI 1264/26. These records primarily concern administrative handling, consideration, review and correspondence created for the purpose of responding to the request. The fact that you are identified as the requester does not mean that the records themselves become your personal data. The records principally concern the Force's response process rather than information held about you.
Where personal data relating to you is present within those records, it is closely intertwined with personal data relating to staff members and with internal discussions, advice and deliberations. Meaningful disclosure is not possible without adversely affecting the rights and freedoms of other individuals. Article 15(4) UK GDPR therefore applies.

Correspondence relating to your email of 28 September 2026
We have identified records relating to the receipt, assessment and handling of your email dated 28 September 2026. These records primarily comprise internal communications and administrative actions taken in response to that correspondence.
To the extent that personal data relating to you is contained within those records, it is incidental to their primary purpose and cannot be meaningfully separated from information relating to other individuals and internal deliberative material. Disclosure would adversely affect the rights and freedoms of other individuals and is therefore restricted under Article 15(4) UK GDPR.

Internal emails, notes and communications referring to you
We have also considered your request for any internal emails, notes, discussions, comments, assessments or correspondence referring to you during the relevant period. To the extent that such records exist, they primarily comprise internal administrative material, staff communications and case management records created for operational and administrative purposes.
The fact that an individual is named or referred to within a record does not automatically entitle them to a copy of that record under Article 15 UK GDPR. A subject access request provides a right of access to personal data, not a right of access to documents.
Where such records contain personal data relating to you, that information is inseparable from personal data relating to staff members and other individuals and from internal discussions, advice, opinions and deliberations. We have considered whether any personal data can reasonably be extracted and disclosed in a meaningful form. We are satisfied that it cannot. Disclosure would adversely affect the rights and freedoms of other individuals and is therefore restricted by Article 15(4) UK GDPR.

Outcome
Having completed a proportionate review of the information within scope of this element of your request, we are satisfied that:
• The records requested primarily comprise administrative and internal case handling material concerning the Force's management of requests and correspondence.
• Article 15 UK GDPR does not provide a general right of access to such records or to the documents in which personal data may appear.
• Any personal data relating to you within those records is limited and incidental to their primary purpose.
• Any such personal data cannot be extracted and disclosed in a meaningful manner without disclosing personal data relating to other individuals and adversely affecting their rights and freedoms.
• Article 15(4) UK GDPR applies to any personal data relating to you contained within those records.
Accordingly, no further information will be disclosed in response to this element of your subject access request.
